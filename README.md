# StayCheck

> A second opinion before you book.

StayCheck is a Chrome extension that helps travelers cross-check a shortlisted hotel on Booking.com with its Google Maps listing before booking.

Instead of telling you whether a hotel is "good" or "bad", StayCheck surfaces differences between the two platforms so you can make a more informed decision.

## What it checks

- Google rating and review count
- Matched hotel address and location
- Match confidence between Booking.com and Google
- Up to 5 Google review excerpts
- Direct link to the Google Maps listing
- Clear explanation of how Booking.com and Google ratings differ

## Privacy-first by design

StayCheck only checks a hotel when you explicitly request it.

It does not:

- Automatically scan hotel listings
- Track your browsing history
- Require an account
- Rank hotels based on your browsing activity
- Store Google review text

## Status

🚧 Private beta / prototype

The initial version is being developed for Chrome and Booking.com hotel listings. Public in-page integration is permission-gated.

If Booking.com integration cannot be launched publicly, StayCheck can operate as a standalone hotel name/address checker.

## Philosophy

**Compare, don't average.**

Booking.com and Google represent different reviewer populations, scoring systems, and review contexts. Neither is treated as the absolute source of truth.

StayCheck simply makes the comparison easier.
