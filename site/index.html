How it works

A clearer picture in three simple steps.

StayCheck is designed to add context to your research—not to interrupt it or change how you book.

01

Open a property page

Browse a hotel on a supported Booking.com, Agoda, or Expedia property page as usual.

02

Confirm the right place

StayCheck matches the hotel name and address. If the match is uncertain, you choose the correct listing.

03

Compare the context

See the Google Maps rating, review count, and attributed excerpts before deciding whether to book.
